Purpose & Scope

This framework manages the full lifecycle of Misk Foundation's NCNP regulatory compliance — from registering regulatory obligations through to evidence collection, submission, and closure. Scoped exclusively to NCNP regulations.

Key Principle: The Compliance Officer manually identifies, registers, and maintains all regulatory obligations. The system is a management tool, not a regulatory scanner.

How the System Works

  • Compliance Officer registers each NCNP regulation into the system
  • System creates tasks, assigns owners, sets deadlines, and sends reminders
  • Task owners upload evidence; Compliance Officer reviews and approves
  • Submissions to NCNP are tracked; system logs acceptance or rejection
  • All domains, activities, and controls are fully customizable — no recoding needed

Process Workflow

End-to-end compliance lifecycle. The Compliance Officer drives all regulatory knowledge; the system manages tasks, deadlines, and evidence.

1

Regulation Registration

The Compliance Officer identifies a new or updated NCNP regulation and manually registers it — domain, description, frequency, evidence, deadline, owner.

2

Task Generation & Assignment

System creates a compliance task, auto-assigns to the designated owner, sets the deadline, and sends notification.

3

Evidence Collection

Task owner uploads required evidence. System checks completeness against the checklist defined during registration.

Review Gate — Evidence Complete?

Compliance Officer reviews. Incomplete → return to Step 3. Complete → proceed.

4

Internal Approval

Compliance Officer approves. High-impact items escalate to CEO/Board for secondary sign-off.

5

Submission to NCNP

Submission via NCNP electronic portal. Compliance Officer logs date and reference number.

NCNP Response

Accepted → compliant. Rejected → re-open, loop to Step 3.

6

Closure & Archival

Task marked "Compliant". Evidence archived. Dashboard updated. Recurring items auto-scheduled.

NCNP Regulatory Calendar

NCNP-facing submission deadlines and regulatory milestones only.

Q1: License & Registration Check

January – March

Verify NCNP license validity. Confirm bylaws are current. Update board roster if changes occurred.

Q1: Financial Report Preparation

January – March

Coordinate with Finance on audited statements. Prepare NCNP financial report submission package.

Q2: NCNP Financial & Activity Submissions

April – June

Submit audited financials, annual activity report, and governance report to NCNP. Report General Assembly outcomes.

Q3: Self-Assessment & Governance Evaluation

July – September

Complete and submit NCNP self-assessment. Review fundraising campaign compliance YTD.

Q4: Year-End Compliance Close

October – December

Submit volunteer statistics. Close all NCNP tasks. Archive evidence. Prepare next year's NCNP calendar.

Ongoing: Event-Driven Submissions

Throughout the year

Board change notifications (15 days). Fundraising permits (before campaign). Campaign reports (post-campaign). Bylaws amendments. Activity permits.

System Modules

Platform modules for Misk Foundation's NCNP regulatory compliance management.

Compliance Register

Central database where the Compliance Officer registers all NCNP regulations. Fully customizable domains, controls, and evidence requirements.

Task & Deadline Manager

Auto-generates tasks from registered regulations. Assigns owners, sets deadlines, sends reminders (14, 7, 3 days), escalates overdue items.

Evidence Vault

Document upload, versioning, archival. Validates completeness against checklists. Searchable and exportable.

NCNP Submission Tracker

Logs all NCNP submissions — date, reference, status (pending/accepted/rejected). Single view of all NCNP interactions.
